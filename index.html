🇪🇪 Estonia · EPL leagues

Stages of the Eesti Padeli Liit season. Each league has 7 stages a year (women's B has 6). Who may enter depends on the EPL ranking, which counts a player's best 5 stages of the last 12 months.

A-liiga
Top league, open to everyone. If oversubscribed, pairs get in by ranking. Winner earns 500 points, and the Estonian Championship stage 750.
B-liiga
Amateur league. The top 30 men (or women) of the ranking may not enter. Winner earns 100 points. B players may still play A stages.
C-liiga
The top 70 men may not enter. Short pro-set matches to 8. Winner earns 50 points.
D-liiga
Entry league. The top 120 men may not enter, new players without ranking points are welcome, places go by registration order. Winner earns 25 points.

Source: EPL competition regulations 2026. This site lists A and B only.

🇱🇻 Latvia · LPF series

Tournaments of the Latvijas Padel Federācija ranking series (LPF reitinga sacensības). The tag on each tournament is its tier; inside a tournament players enter by level.

Gold
Top tier of the series, often with a prize fund. Usually offers levels A to D for men and women.
Silver
Second tier, same level structure.
Bronze
Third tier. Currently used for the women's league stages, levels B to D.
Levels A–D
A is the strongest group and D the entry group. PRO (A) and AM (B–D) are used at festivals.
Championship
Latvijas Republikas padel čempionāts, one open men's and women's draw.

Derived from the categories published on padelfederacija.lv. The federation has not published written level criteria on its site.

🇫🇮 Finland · SPL classes

Players are placed in a class by their position in the Suomen Padelliitto ranking (best 7 results of the last 12 months). Class codes are prefixed M for men and N for women, so MC1 is men's C1. P and T with an age are boys' and girls' junior classes, M40 or N40 are age groups.

A1 · A2
Ranking 1–15 and 16–60 for men. Elite and advanced.
B1 · B2
Ranking 61–115 and 116–200. Upper intermediate.
C1 · C2
Ranking 201–400 and 401–700. Intermediate.
D1 · D2
Ranking 701–1000 and 1001–2000. Beginner competitive.
E
Ranking 2001 and beyond, or no ranking yet. Entry class.

Women use the same nine classes with lower position thresholds.

FPT Gold
Finnish Padel Tour main events, 4 a year. Winner earns 1000 ranking points, prize money. Usually paired with the Babolat Junior Tour (BJT).
FPT Silver
Finnish Padel Tour, 4 a year, lighter format. Winner earns 800 points.
Nationals
Kansalliset kilpailut: club-run ranking tournaments played per class, the bulk of the calendar.

Sources: padel.fi ranking classes 2026, finnishpadeltour.fi.

🌍 FIP Bronze

The Cupra FIP Tour is the International Padel Federation's professional circuit with four tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum. Bronze is the entry tier, the most common way to earn first FIP ranking points, and the one this site tracks.

Source: padelfip.com calendar.
